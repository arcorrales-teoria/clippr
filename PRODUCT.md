# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Content creators, podcasters, educators, and conference organizers who need to repurpose longer-form video for short-form social media (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts). They are comfortable with technical tools, value their time, and want a result they can post immediately — not a new skill to learn. They run this locally: no upload delays, no subscription, no data leaving their machine.

## Product Purpose

clippr converts any landscape or portrait video into vertical 9:16 clips. It detects who is speaking in each frame, follows them automatically, and burns in word-level karaoke captions. The output is a folder of ready-to-post clips, with zero manual timeline editing required. Success means the user drops a video in and walks away with clips they're proud to publish.

## Brand Personality

Effortless, smart, clean. The AI does the heavy lifting; the interface stays composed. Calm confidence over showmanship. The product's intelligence should be felt in the results, not announced in the UI.

## Anti-references

- **Consumer SaaS warm-neutral**: No cream, sand, or beige backgrounds. No warm-tinted near-whites. No "paper" or "linen" surfaces.
- **Adobe / Premiere / DaVinci**: No heavy panel chrome, no sprawling toolbars, no pro-video editor density. Complexity lives in the settings drawer, not the default view.
- **Generic AI tool aesthetics**: No gradient hero orbs, no "seamless" or "effortless" headline copy, no floating glass cards used as decoration.

## Design Principles

1. **Local-first, no ceremony.** The tool runs on your machine; the interface should feel just as direct. No onboarding funnels, no upsells, no distractions between the user and their output.
2. **Show the machine working.** AI processing is the core value proposition. Progress should be visible, specific, and trust-building — not hidden behind a spinner.
3. **Defaults are smart; options are available.** Advanced settings exist but don't demand attention. The surface should work well for someone who never opens the settings panel.
4. **Output is the reward.** The clip grid is the payoff. Design toward that moment. Every earlier step should feel like progress toward it.
5. **Precision without weight.** The interface handles technically complex work. Keep visual weight minimal so the content — the video, the speakers, the clips — is always the loudest thing on screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Focus-visible outlines on all interactive elements. No color-only status signals (always pair with icon or text). Reduced motion alternatives for all transitions and progress animations. Sufficient contrast on muted/secondary text against dark surfaces (minimum 4.5:1 for body, 3:1 for large text).
